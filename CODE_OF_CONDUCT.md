# Code of Conduct

**Quick version: Don't be an asshole. Respect people. Ship good code.**

---

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Be respectful in discussions
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on technical merit
- Help newcomers
- Assume good intent

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment or discrimination
- Trolling or inflammatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Anything that would get you fired from a reasonable workplace

**In code:**
- Write clear, maintainable code
- Document complex logic
- Test your changes
- Don't introduce security vulnerabilities
- Respect the style guide

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct. They have the right to:
- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions
- Ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for violations

**Process:**
1. Warning for first minor offense
2. Temporary ban for repeat offenses or major violations
3. Permanent ban for continued unacceptable behavior

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

**Email**: conduct@[domain].com
**Anonymous**: Use [this form](https://forms.example.com/report)

Reports will be reviewed within 48 hours. We will respect confidentiality requests within legal limits.
